High School GPA Calculator

A High School GPA Calculator helps students quickly calculate their grade point average using course grades, credit hours, and a standard grading scale. It converts letter grades like A, B, and C into grade points and then calculates a weighted average based on each subject’s importance. This makes it easier to track academic performance, plan for college admissions, and understand how different classes, assignments, and exams affect your overall GPA.
