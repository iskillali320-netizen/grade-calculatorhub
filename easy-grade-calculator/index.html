Easy Grade Calculator

Our Easy Grade Calculator is the quickest way to find your test score. Whether you're a student checking a quiz result or a teacher grading papers, simply enter the total number of questions and how many were answered correctly to instantly get the percentage score and letter grade.

How to Use This Calculator

1
Enter Total Questions: Count the total number of questions or points possible on the assignment.
2
Enter Correct Answers: Input the number of questions you got right (or the total points earned).
3
Get Your Grade: The calculator instantly shows your percentage score and the corresponding letter grade.

Example Calculation

Imagine you just took a history test with 50 questions in total, and you answered 42 of them correctly.

Total Questions: 50

Correct Answers: 42

Result: Your score is 84%, which is a solid B.

The Percentage Formula

If you want to calculate your test grade manually, the formula is very simple:

Percentage = (Correct Answers / Total Questions) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

What grading scale does this tool use?

It uses the standard US 10-point grading scale: A (90-100%), B (80-89%), C (70-79%), D (60-69%), and F (below 60%).

What if my test questions have different point values?

If some questions are worth more than others, do not enter the number of questions. Instead, enter the Total Points Possible in the first box, and the Points Earned in the second box.

Can I calculate my grade using the number of wrong answers?

Yes! Just subtract your wrong answers from the total questions to find your correct answers. For example, on a 20-question test with 3 wrong answers, your correct answers would be 17.

Is an 85% a good grade?

Generally, yes! An 85% is a B, which is above average and typically considered a strong academic performance.

Does this calculator work for half-points?

Yes, our calculator supports decimals. If you earned 42.5 points out of 50, you can enter exactly that for an accurate result.
