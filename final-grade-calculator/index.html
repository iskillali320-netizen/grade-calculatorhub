What Grade Do I Need on My Final?

Our Final Grade Calculator takes the guesswork out of finals week. Simply enter your current class grade, the final grade you want to achieve, and how much your final exam is worth. We'll instantly calculate the exact score you need on your final to reach your goal.

How to Use This Calculator

1
Enter your current grade: This is your overall grade in the class right now (e.g., 85%).
2
Enter your desired class grade: The final overall grade you want on your report card or transcript (e.g., 90%).
3
Enter the final exam weight: How much of your total grade the final exam represents (e.g., 20%).

Example Calculation

Let's say you currently have an 82% in your Math class. You really want to get an A- (90%) for the semester. Your teacher said the final exam is worth 25% of your total grade.

Current Grade: 82%

Desired Grade: 90%

Exam Weight: 25%

Result: You need to score at least 114% on your final exam.

(Since this is over 100%, you would need extra credit to reach your goal. It might be better to lower your target to a B+ to see a realistic score!)

The Final Grade Formula

If you prefer to do the math yourself, here is the standard formula used to calculate the required final exam score:

Final Exam Score = [ Target Grade - (Current Grade × (1 - Weight)) ] / Weight

Note: The "Weight" must be converted to a decimal first (e.g., 20% becomes 0.20).

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean if my required score is over 100%?

If the calculator tells you that you need over a 100%, it means it is mathematically impossible to reach your desired class grade based on the weight of the final exam, unless your teacher offers significant extra credit.

What if my required score is negative?

A negative score means you have already locked in your desired grade! Even if you score a zero on the final exam, your current grade is high enough to keep you at or above your target.

How do I find out my final exam weight?

Your final exam weight is usually listed in your class syllabus. If it's not there, ask your teacher or professor. Common weights are 15%, 20%, or 25%.

Does this work for college classes?

Yes! This calculator works for high school, college, and middle school classes as long as your grading system uses percentages.

What if my class uses a points system instead of weights?

If your class uses a total points system, this specific calculator won't work perfectly. You should instead add up your total points earned and divide by the total points possible.
