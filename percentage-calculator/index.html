What is a Percentage Calculator?

Our Percentage Calculator is a versatile tool that helps you instantly figure out what percentage one number is of another. While it's perfect for finding your grade on a test, it's also incredibly useful for calculating store discounts, analyzing statistical data, or solving everyday math problems without needing to remember the formulas.

How to Use This Calculator

1
Enter the Partial Amount: In the first box, enter the specific portion or part you have. For a test, this would be the number of questions you got right.
2
Enter the Total Amount: In the second box, enter the whole amount. For a test, this is the total number of questions.
3
Get Your Result: Click calculate to instantly see the exact percentage.

Example Calculation

Let's say you took a Science quiz. There were 45 questions on the quiz, and you answered 38 of them correctly.

Partial Amount (Got Right): 38

Total Amount (Questions): 45

Result: 38 is exactly 84.44% of 45.

The Percentage Formula

To find the percentage manually, you simply divide the partial amount by the total amount, and then multiply by 100.

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "percent" actually mean?

The word "percent" comes from the Latin phrase per centum, which literally translates to "by the hundred." So, when you say 85%, you are saying 85 out of every 100.

Can the partial amount be larger than the total amount?

Yes! If the partial amount is larger than the total amount, your result will be over 100%. For example, if you earned 110 points on a test that was only worth 100 points (due to extra credit), 110 is 110% of 100.

Does this work for money and discounts?

Absolutely. If a shirt originally costs $50 (Total Amount) and is on sale for $40 (Partial Amount), you can use this calculator to find out that you are paying 80% of the original price (meaning you got a 20% discount).
