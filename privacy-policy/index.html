Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

At Grade Calculator, your privacy is a top priority. We've designed our website so that it's safe, simple, and transparent.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal data when you use our calculators. Any grades, scores, or numbers you enter stay entirely on your device and are never sent to our servers.

We use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience and occasionally to serve display ads. These cookies do not track personal identifying information like your name or email. You can disable cookies in your browser settings anytime.

Our website may contain links to helpful third-party sites. Once you leave our site, we are no longer responsible for your privacy on those domains. We encourage you to read their privacy policies as well.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
