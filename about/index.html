About Us

Welcome to Grade Calculator!

We know how stressful school can be. Between exams, homework, and projects, trying to figure out your final grade or GPA often feels like a complicated math problem you didn't sign up for.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to help students take control of their academic journey. We build clean, fast, and completely free calculating tools so you never have to guess where you stand in a class.

Why We Built This

Grade Calculator was built for students, by students. We realized that existing tools were often clunky, outdated, or confusing. We wanted to create a modern, easy-to-use platform that gives you accurate results instantly—no sign-ups required, and no complicated formulas to memorize.

Let's Connect

We are constantly looking to improve. If you have a suggestion for a new calculator or just want to say hello, we'd love to hear from you. Head over to our Contact page to drop us a message!
