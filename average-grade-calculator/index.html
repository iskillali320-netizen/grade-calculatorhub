Average Grade Calculator

Our Average Grade Calculator helps you quickly find your unweighted mean class score. Whether you're tracking homework, quizzes, or tests, simply input your grades to instantly see your overall average. It's the perfect tool for students who want to stay on top of their academic progress without doing tedious math.

How to Use This Calculator

1
Enter your grades: Type in the percentage score for each assignment or test (e.g., 85, 92, 78).
2
Add more rows if needed: Click the "+ Add Another Assignment" button to include as many grades as you want.
3
Calculate: Hit the "Calculate Average" button to instantly see your unweighted mean grade.

Example Calculation

Let's say you have taken three quizzes so far this semester, and they are all worth the same amount of points.

Quiz 1: 85%

Quiz 2: 92%

Quiz 3: 78%

Result: The calculator adds these up (255) and divides by 3. Your average grade is exactly 85%.

The Average Grade Formula

If you need to calculate an unweighted average manually, here is the basic formula:

Average Grade = (Sum of All Grades) / (Total Number of Grades)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an unweighted average?

An unweighted average means every assignment or test you enter has the exact same value or "weight" towards your final grade. A 10-point homework assignment affects your grade just as much as a 100-point test in an unweighted system.

What if my classes use weighted grades?

If your syllabus says that Homework is 20% and Tests are 50%, you cannot use a simple average calculator for your final grade. However, you can still use this tool to find your average within a specific category (like finding your average test score before applying the weight).

Can I enter points instead of percentages?

Yes! If all your assignments are out of 100 points, entering points is exactly the same as entering percentages. If they are out of different point values, you should calculate the percentage for each one first, or use a total points calculator.

How many grades can I add?

You can add an unlimited number of grades. Just keep clicking the "+ Add Another Assignment" button.
